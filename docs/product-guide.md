# Masting product guide

[← Back to the showcase](../README.md)

Masting brings professional mailboxes, company supervision and managed domains into one corporate email workspace.

## Start with the company

1. [Request access](https://masting.ai/signup) and provide company information.
2. Establish the approved office-network access policy.
3. Start the 14-day subscription trial.
4. Add the required domain combo and fund the prepaid wallet for chargeable services.
5. Let the Masting team complete domain registration, configuration and routing.
6. Add people, assign roles and set up mailboxes.

The trial does not make domains free. A domain request is not a reservation on the public market.

## Everyday email

The mailbox includes folders, threaded correspondence, labels, search and contacts. People can compose, reply, reply-all, forward, attach files, use templates and signatures, and schedule a message.

An Undo Send window applies before submission. A message that is already accepted by an external mail server cannot be recalled merely by canceling the local outbox entry.

## Sending and status

Recipient checks and suppression rules are part of sending. A risky result requires an explicit acknowledgement. A blocked recipient cannot be made sendable by the absence of a daily plan cap.

Delivery status distinguishes queued work, submission, SMTP acceptance and failure or uncertainty. **SMTP acceptance does not prove inbox placement.** Ambiguous outcomes are not automatically resent, which helps avoid duplicate deliveries.

## Team supervision

Company owners and managers have a company-wide mail view. Opening a message through supervision is audited and does not change the agent's own read state. A company can organize access by role rather than sharing one login.

Masting is therefore not an end-to-end encrypted personal messenger: company management has visibility by design.

## Company operations

The company workspace brings together:

- People, roles and mailboxes.
- Domains and provisioning.
- Branding, signatures and templates.
- Wallet, invoices and billing history.
- Access policy and activity information.
- Message-linked issue reports and delivery diagnostics.

Platform operators handle provisioning and support operations. This public repository intentionally excludes internal deployment procedures, administrative access details and private application code.

## Scope

Masting is for lawful company correspondence. There are no campaign builders, contact-list uploads, mail merges or bulk-send APIs. Optional integrations and their current availability should be confirmed during onboarding.
