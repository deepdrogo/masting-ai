# Frequently asked questions

[← Back to the showcase](../README.md)

## What is Masting?

A corporate email workspace with professional mailboxes, company supervision, approved-network access, managed domains and prepaid billing.

## Is this a bulk-email or marketing platform?

No. Masting has no campaigns, list uploads, mail merges or bulk-send APIs. It is for lawful business correspondence.

## What does it cost?

$999 per company per month after a 14-day subscription trial. Each three-domain combo is another $399 per month, including registration. Taxes are excluded. One combo plus the subscription totals $1,398 per month after the trial.

## Is everything free for the first 14 days?

No. The trial waives the subscription fee only. Domain combos are charged during the trial.

## Are people and mailboxes charged separately?

The standard plan includes unlimited people and mailboxes, without a per-seat subscription charge.

## Does “no daily sending caps” mean unrestricted bulk mail?

No. It describes the standard plan's daily allowance. Recipient checks, suppression, access controls, acceptable-use rules and external provider constraints still apply.

## How many domains can a company have?

The published limit is up to 10 domains. Standard combos contain three domains; check nonstandard arrangements with the team.

## Does submitting a domain request register it?

No. Registration and provisioning are handled by Masting. A request is not a public-market reservation.

## Can managers see employees' mail?

Yes. Company supervision is a core feature. Supervisory reads are audited and do not change the agent's read state. This is not an end-to-end encrypted personal mailbox.

## Does office-only access prove someone is in the office?

No. It means the connection comes from an approved network. An approved VPN exit may be reachable remotely.

## What happens if the wallet cannot cover a monthly charge?

The company is paused, not deleted. It resumes when sufficient funding covers outstanding charges.

## Are AI assistance and automated crypto top-ups available?

Optional integrations must be confirmed with the Masting team. This showcase does not market them as currently active launch features.

## Where can I get the source code or self-hosting package?

This repository is a product showcase, not an open-source application release. The Masting implementation remains private.
