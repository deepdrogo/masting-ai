# Pricing and billing

[← Back to the showcase](../README.md)

Checked October 30, 2026 against the [published pricing](https://masting.ai/pricing). All amounts are USD and exclude taxes.

## One company plan

**$999 per company per month**, after a **14-day subscription trial**.

The plan includes unlimited people and mailboxes. There is no per-seat subscription charge and no daily sending cap under the standard plan. Access controls, recipient verification, suppression, acceptable-use rules and external provider constraints still apply.

## Domain combos

**$399 per combo per month.**

Each combo contains three domains: one primary and two alternatives. Registration is included in the combo price. Domains are registered and maintained through the Masting team, with charges taken from the prepaid wallet.

Domain charges apply **during the trial too**. The 14-day trial waives only the $999 subscription fee.

The published company limit is up to 10 domains. Combos are sold in threes. Confirm an arrangement outside complete combos with Masting rather than assuming a fourth combo fits the published limit.

## Prepaid wallet

Billing uses a prepaid USD wallet with transaction history and invoices. Confirm currently available funding methods with the team during onboarding. This guide does not promise that automated cryptocurrency top-ups are enabled.

If the wallet cannot cover a monthly payment, the company is paused; its data is not deleted. Once sufficient funding covers outstanding charges, access resumes.

## Before purchase

- Check the [live pricing page](https://masting.ai/pricing).
- Confirm domain availability and any provisioning conditions with Masting.
- Review the [Terms of Service](https://masting.ai/legal/terms) and [Refund Policy](https://masting.ai/legal/refunds).
- Do not treat this showcase as a binding quote, tax calculation or refund promise.

Older Business/Unlimited tiers, per-seat prices and paid daily overage blocks are not the standard pricing presented here.
